\reflection{Learning Unfamiliar Domains Through Conversation and Curiosity}{Solutions Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist}

Over the past decade, I have gained expertise spanning physics-based research, power line design, and software development. This unusual background is what defines my strength in solutions engineering: while I am not the world's leading expert on a tightly defined subject, I lean on conversation and curiosity to gain the level of expertise needed to be a reliable asset.

When I sit with individuals and teams from different backgrounds, I start by drawing out their own sense of value and expertise. I then pull from my past experiences of problem breakdown to tackle what needs to be learned within a new domain. Applying this pattern has allowed me to amplify my business connection with other teams and shape solutions that address their pain points and become a component of their business excellence.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\reflection{Breaking Down Complex Problems Across Scales}{Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Deployment Strategist}

My PhD research, focused on multi-physics material behavior, taught me how to break complex problems into smaller, approachable pieces. It taught me how a highly detailed focus on a problem and the big picture can be reconciled into cohesive, multi-scale insight, and it taught me how to absorb knowledge at different scales, efficiently and usefully.

I applied these skills in the transmission engineering role I took on next, where I quickly picked up design principles and addressed practical challenges.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\reflection{Bridging Subject-Matter Experts and Technology}{Solutions Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist}

I have been recognized as a high-performance transmission line engineer and an engineer making a difference for customers while also seeking out and teaming with others to improve business components outside my direct role. My interests and skills then drew me to a Reliability and Standards engineering role, where my engineering communication and multifaceted background enabled me to design an end-to-end, enterprise-grade ecosystem that meshes granular, equipment-level electrical calculations and system-level data analysis insights on a foundation of user-efficient data management.

My experience in current and past roles has helped me understand the value that can be created by purposefully synthesizing otherwise independent skills to become an effective and reliable bridge between subject-matter experts and technology infrastructure. I believe this quality is what drives great solutions engineers, forward deployed engineers, and deployment strategists to excellence, and I hold the determination to take my career to the next level along this path.
