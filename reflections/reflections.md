Professional Reflections

## Learning Unfamiliar Domains Through Conversation and Curiosity

*Supports: Solutions Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist*

Over the past decade, I have gained expertise spanning physics-based research, power line design, and software development. This unusual background is what defines my strength in solutions engineering: there are many fields where I am not the world's leading expert, but through conversation and curiosity I gain the level of expertise needed to be a reliable asset.

When I sit with individuals and teams from different backgrounds, I start by drawing out their own sense of value and expertise. I then pull from my past experiences of gaining expertise and creating value to tackle the things I need to learn, so that I can strengthen the mutual connection with a client and shape solutions that address their pain points and become a component of their business excellence.

## Breaking Down Complex Problems Across Scales

*Supports: Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Deployment Strategist*

My PhD research, focused on the mechanics of materials, taught me how to break complex problems into smaller, approachable pieces. It taught me how a highly detailed focus on a problem and the big picture can be reconciled into cohesive, multi-scale insight, and it taught me how to absorb knowledge efficiently and usefully.

I applied these skills in the transmission engineering role I took on next, where I quickly picked up design principles and their practical challenges.

---

## Bridging Subject-Matter Experts and Technology

*Supports: Solutions Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Deployment Strategist*

I was recognized as a high-performance transmission line engineer while also seeking out and teaming with others to improve business components outside my direct role. My interests and skills then drew me to a Reliability and Standards engineering role, where I combined many of the skills I had gained to design an end-to-end, enterprise-grade ecosystem that meshes granular, equipment-level electrical calculations and system-level data analysis insights on a foundation of user-efficient data management.

My experience in current and past roles has helped me understand the value that can be created by purposefully synthesizing otherwise independent skills to become an effective and reliable bridge between subject-matter experts and technology infrastructure. I believe this lens is what drives great solutions engineers, forward deployed engineers, and deployment strategists to excellence, and I feel the determination to take my career to the next level along this path.
